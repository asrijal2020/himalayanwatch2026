    Approved observations

    Quality checks

    Validate units, timing, provenance, uncertainty, semantics, and synthetic status.

    Evidence storage

    Retain immutable payload hashes, acquisition receipts, and observation/availability clocks.

    Scientific review

    Delivery and action

    Track retries, receipt confirmation, community action, and usable warning time.
